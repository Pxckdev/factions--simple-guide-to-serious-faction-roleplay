Simple Guide To Serious Faction Roleplay

Faction RP is more than shootouts. Here's how to keep your hood alive between the action.

Things to do in faction RP

Bored in the hood? Pick something from this list and roleplay it with your people.

  • Taking trips to get food
  • Working out
  • Boosting stolen goods
  • Doing tattoos
  • Cutting hair as a barber
  • Car repairs
  • Breaking into cars / joyriding
  • Getting high with gang members and friends
  • Selling drugs, with or without help from your friends
  • Doing strike-ups (graffiti). You can roleplay being experienced or a novice.
  • Gambling
  • Running fades
  • Trooping / patrolling the hood
  • Taking trips to different spots in Vinewood. Remember that Vinewood is based on Hollywood, so there are lots of tourist-attraction-type places you can RP at.
  • Attending parties in other hoods
  • Hosting parties
  • Scamming (stolen cards, ATM skimmers, fraud, etc.)
  • Cleaning guns / adding modifications
  • Flocking / house robberies, in areas that aren't obviously gang turf. Robbing on someone's turf could get you attacked by that faction.

Nothing to talk about?

If you find yourself with nothing to talk about in-game, here are a few topics:

Character story topics (that would be common knowledge) School Sports Politics in other areas / gangs Home life Women Drugs Enemies Money schemes Music Rumors in the neighborhood
